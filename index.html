Install SmartTank App

Add SmartTank to your phone home screen for 1-tap fast access like a native app without typing URLs!

Android (Chrome / Edge / Samsung)
  1. Tap the Three Dots (⋮) in Chrome top-right.
  2. Tap "Install App" (or "Add to Home screen").
  3. Tap "Install". SmartTank icon will appear on your phone!
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share Button (⎋) at the bottom.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen (⊞)".
  3. Tap "Add" in the top right.
